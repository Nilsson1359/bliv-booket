Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data Fuldt Booket Musiker collects when you visit our website, apply for our programme or become a client, what we use it for, and the rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Data controller

Fuldt Booket Musiker (Bliv Booket™ / Fully Booked Musician™)

Website: fuldtbooketmusiker.dk

Contact: booking@oscartjoensson.dk

1. What we collect

When you visit the website

  • Usage data: pages and sections viewed, clicks, scroll depth, time on page, referring site and campaign parameters (for example from an ad).
  • Technical data: IP address, approximate location (city and country), device, browser and screen size.
  • Cookies and similar technologies from Meta (the Meta Pixel) and Google Tag Manager, used to measure and improve our advertising. See section 5.

When you apply or book a call

  • Name, email address and phone number.
  • Your answers in the application form, such as how many paid gigs you play, your current and desired fee per gig, your yearly gig goal, whether you play covers or original material, your monthly music income and investment budget. Answers are saved as you go, also before you submit.
  • The time you choose for your strategy call, and notes from the call.

When you are a client

  • Information needed to deliver the programme and book gigs for you, such as your services, prices, availability and contact details of the clients who book you.
  • If you choose to connect your Google Calendar: calendar data as described in section 4.
  • Billing and payment information required by Danish bookkeeping law.

3. Who processes data for us

We use trusted providers (data processors) that only process data on our instructions:

  • Cloudflare: hosting of the website, video and our analytics database.
  • HighLevel / LeadConnector: our CRM, where applications, contact details and booked calls are stored.
  • Google: Google Meet for video calls, Google Tag Manager, Google Fonts, and the Google Calendar connection described below.
  • Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram): ad measurement via the Meta Pixel and the Conversions API.
  • Telegram: internal notifications to our team when a new application arrives.

Some of these providers are based in, or may transfer data to, the United States. Such transfers are based on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

4. Google Calendar and Google user data

As a client you can choose to connect your Google Calendar to Fuldt Booket Musiker with Google sign-in. This is optional, and you can disconnect at any time.

What we access

  • Your free/busy information and the start and end times of events, so we can see whether you are available before we confirm a gig for you.
  • The ability to create, update and delete events for gigs we book for you, so they appear in your calendar automatically.
  • Your Google account email address, to identify which calendar is connected.

How we use it

  • Only to check your availability and to keep gigs booked through us in sync with your calendar.
  • We do not read, store or share the content of your other events beyond what is needed to know when you are busy.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.
  • Humans at Fuldt Booket Musiker do not read your calendar data, except with your explicit permission, when needed for security reasons or to comply with law.

Storage, retention and revoking access

  • Access tokens are stored encrypted and are only used by our booking system.
  • When you disconnect, or ask us to delete your data, we delete your tokens and any stored calendar data within 30 days.
  • You can revoke access at any time in your Google Account under Third-party apps & services.

Our own sales team (staff only)

Members of our own sales team can also connect their work Google account. This is not offered to clients or musicians. For staff we access:

  • Read-only access to their own calendar events, to find the Google Meet link of a sales meeting they hold with a prospective client.
  • Read-only information about those Google Meet meetings (who joined and for how long), so our system can register whether the meeting took place, instead of the salesperson marking it by hand.

This data is used only inside our internal system, under the same rules as above: it is not sold, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.

Fuldt Booket Musiker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Cookies and advertising

We advertise on Facebook and Instagram. To measure whether our ads work, the website uses the Meta Pixel, and we send certain events (such as a completed application or booked call) to Meta through the Conversions API. Contact details sent this way are hashed (encrypted one-way) before they leave our server. Meta may use this data to show you relevant ads in line with Meta's Privacy Policy.

You can limit ad tracking in your browser settings and in your Meta ad preferences. Our own website statistics do not use advertising cookies.

6. How long we keep data

  • Applications and contact details: as long as needed to handle your application and stay in contact, and no longer than 24 months after our last contact if you do not become a client.
  • Client data: for the duration of our collaboration, and accounting records for 5 years as required by the Danish Bookkeeping Act.
  • Website statistics: up to 13 months.
  • Google Calendar tokens and data: until you disconnect or ask us to delete them (see section 4).

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw any consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before.

To use your rights, email booking@oscartjoensson.dk. We respond within 30 days.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), or to the data protection authority in your own country.

8. Security

All traffic to the website is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to personal data is limited to the people who need it to do their job, and our providers are bound by data processing agreements.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available on this page.
